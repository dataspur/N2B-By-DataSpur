# BullshID Pilot Program
## 3-Month Validation with Premier Texas Venues

---

## Executive Summary

BullshID is launching a **3-month pilot program** with three premier Texas rodeo/country music venues to validate our AI-powered fake ID detection system before national rollout. This program provides **free access** to cutting-edge technology in exchange for usage data, feedback, and testimonials that will shape our product roadmap.

**Pilot Venues:**
1. **Billy Bob's Texas** (Fort Worth) - 6,000 capacity, world's largest honky-tonk
2. **Second Rodeo** (Fort Worth) - Upscale nightlife, mid-volume venue
3. **Koe Wetzel's Riot Room** (Fort Worth & Houston) - Multi-location chain

**Value Proposition for Venues:**
- **$0 cost** for 3-month pilot (normally $299-499/month)
- Free iPad Pro kits with UV/IR camera filters ($500/device value)
- 20% discount on first-year subscription after pilot
- Priority access to hyperspectral features
- Legal affirmative defense under Texas TBC §106.14
- Insurance premium reduction documentation support

**Value for BullshID:**
- Real-world validation at 3 high-volume venues (5,000+ scans/week combined)
- ML training data from diverse ID types (TX residents + tourists)
- Bouncer UX feedback for product optimization
- Case studies and testimonials for sales materials
- Multi-location testing (Riot Room Fort Worth + Houston)

## Pilot Program Structure

### Phase 1: Onboarding (Weeks 1-2)

#### Week 1: Setup & Training
**Deliverables:**
- BullshID app deployed to venue-provided or BullshID-loaned devices
- Virtual training sessions (1 hour per venue via Zoom)
- Printed quick-start guides for door staff
- Dedicated Slack channel for real-time support

**Device Allocation:**
| Venue | Devices | Hardware Type | Justification |
|-------|---------|--------------|---------------|
| Billy Bob's Texas | 5 iPads | BullshID Pro Kit (UV/IR filters) | High-volume, multiple entry points |
| Second Rodeo | 2 iPads | Standard iPad + app | Mid-volume, 1-2 doors |
| Riot Room (FTW) | 3 iPads | BullshID Pro Kit | Multi-door testing |
| Riot Room (HTX) | 3 iPads | BullshID Pro Kit | Multi-location sync validation |
| **Total** | **13 devices** | **$6,500 value** | |

**Training Agenda (1 Hour):**
1. App overview and navigation (10 min)
2. Scanning workflow: Front → Back → Selfie (15 min)
3. Interpreting results: Pass/Fail/Alert states (10 min)
4. Offline mode and data sync (10 min)
5. Troubleshooting common issues (10 min)
6. Q&A (5 min)

**Onboarding Checklist:**
- [ ] Devices shipped and received
- [ ] App installed and activated with venue license keys
- [ ] Training session completed (attendance: manager + 2 bouncers minimum)
- [ ] Slack channel active with venue contact
- [ ] Test scans validated (5 successful scans per device)

#### Week 2: Soft Launch
**Deliverables:**
- Parallel operation: BullshID + existing verification methods
- No reliance on BullshID for final entry decisions (shadowing mode)
- Baseline data collection on current fake ID detection rates

**Goals:**
- Bouncers become comfortable with app workflow
- Identify UX friction points early
- Validate technical stability (uptime, scan speed)

---

### Phase 2: Active Testing (Weeks 3-10)

#### Primary Operation Mode (Weeks 3-8)
**Operational Protocol:**
- BullshID becomes **primary ID verification** tool at all equipped doors
- Bouncers use discretion to override alerts (track override reasons in app)
- Weekly check-in calls (30 min) to review:
  - Detection accuracy
  - False positive/negative rates
  - Bouncer satisfaction scores
  - Technical issues

**Data Collection (Automated):**
For each scan, anonymized metadata logged:
- Timestamp and venue location
- ID type (state, country, document type)
- Scan result: Pass / Fail / Alert (with reason codes)
- Verification time (seconds from scan start to result)
- Bouncer override (yes/no + reason if available)
- Age detected vs. legal threshold (21+)

**Weekly Reporting:**
Every Monday, venues receive:
- **Scan Volume Report**: Total scans, peak hours, busiest doors
- **Detection Summary**: Fake IDs flagged, success rate vs. manual inspection
- **Performance Metrics**: Average scan time, app uptime, error rates
- **Insights**: Top fraud patterns (e.g., "15 fake Texas DLs from same template")

#### Hyperspectral Beta (Weeks 6-10)
**Activation:**
- Select bouncers at Billy Bob's and Riot Room receive hyperspectral training
- UV/IR camera filters activated for hologram/ink analysis
- Side-by-side comparison: Standard scan vs. hyperspectral scan

**Hypothesis:**
Hyperspectral layer will increase fake ID detection by **10-15%** vs. barcode + biometric alone.

**Testing:**
- 100 suspected fake IDs rescanned with hyperspectral mode
- Results compared to manual forensic inspection (ground truth)
- Accuracy, speed, and bouncer usability scored

#### Transition Planning (Week 12)
**Decision Point:**
Venues choose one of three paths:

1. **Full Subscription (Recommended):**
   - Transition to paid tier (Basic/Pro/Enterprise)

2. **Extended Pilot:**
   - Additional 1-2 months free if more data needed

   - Requires commitment to decision by end of extension

3. **Opt-Out:**
   - Return hardware (prepaid shipping labels provided)
   - No cost, no obligation
   - Exit survey to understand reasons (product roadmap feedback)

**Post-Pilot Support:**
- First 30 days: Daily check-ins for paying customers
- Ongoing: 24/7 support via Slack, 4-hour response SLA
- Quarterly business reviews with venue managers

## Pilot Participation Agreement (Summary)

### Venue Commitments
1. **Device Care**: Use iPads for BullshID only, report damage/loss within 24 hours
2. **Data Sharing**: Allow anonymized scan metadata collection (no PII retained)
3. **Training Participation**: Manager + minimum 2 bouncers attend onboarding
4. **Feedback**: Complete weekly check-ins, mid-pilot survey, and final evaluation
5. **Good Faith Testing**: Use BullshID as primary verification tool (not just backup)
6. **Testimonial**: If satisfied, provide quote/case study for marketing (optional but encouraged)

### BullshID Commitments
1. **Free Access**: $0 cost for 3-month pilot (normally $900-1,500 value)
2. **Free Hardware**: Keep iPads after pilot regardless of subscription decision
3. **24/7 Support**: Dedicated Slack channel, 2-hour response SLA
4. **No Lock-In**: Option to opt-out at end of pilot with no penalty
5. **Post-Pilot Discount**: 20% off first-year subscription if converting to paid
6. **Privacy**: Zero PII retention, GDPR/CCPA compliant, data deleted post-pilot if requested

### Legal & Liability
- **Affirmative Defense**: BullshID scanning provides legal protection under Texas TBC §106.14 (venue still responsible for final entry decisions)
- **Insurance**: BullshID carries $2M general liability; not liable for false negatives (missed fake IDs) during pilot
- **Data**: All scan data encrypted in transit and at rest, stored on US-based servers
- **IP**: Venue gets no ownership of BullshID software; BullshID owns anonymized training data

**Pilot Agreement Term**: 12 weeks from activation date, auto-terminates unless subscription signed.

## Risk Mitigation

### Technical Risks
| Risk | Mitigation | Contingency |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| App crashes during peak hours | Pre-launch stress testing, 5,000+ scans | Instant rollback to previous version, manual verification backup |
| Offline mode fails (no sync) | 14-day local storage, background sync retry logic | Extend offline window to 30 days in emergency |
| False positives (legit IDs flagged) | Conservative algorithms, bouncer override always available | Tune sensitivity after Week 2 data analysis |
| Hardware damage/theft | Insurance on all pilot devices | Replacement devices shipped overnight |

### Operational Risks
| Risk | Mitigation | Contingency |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| Bouncers resist new technology | Simple UX, 1-hour training, quick-start cards | On-site training for difficult adopters |
| Entry line slowdowns | 3-second scan target, parallel manual checks during pilot | Shadow mode extension if needed |
| Customer privacy complaints | Clear signage, no PII retention, opt-out selfie mode | Disable face matching if legally challenged |
| Venues drop out mid-pilot | 2 of 4 is sufficient for validation | Recruit replacement venue from waitlist |

### Business Risks
| Risk | Mitigation | Contingency |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| Low conversion to paid (<50%) | Strong ROI demonstration, affirmative defense value | Extend pilot, reduce pricing temporarily |
| Competitor launches similar product | Patents pending on hyperspectral method, first-mover advantage | Accelerate national rollout, lock in Texas market |
| Regulatory changes (age verification laws) | Active monitoring, legal counsel on retainer | Pivot to compliance-focused messaging |
