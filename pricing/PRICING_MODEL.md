# BullshID Pricing Model
## SaaS Subscription Tiers & ROI Framework

---

## Pricing Philosophy

BullshID pricing is designed to:
1. **Align with venue size** - Small bars pay less, large venues pay more
2. **Demonstrate clear ROI** - Every tier pays for itself through fines avoided + insurance savings
3. **Encourage adoption** - Entry price point accessible to all bar types
4. **Scale with value** - Advanced features (hyperspectral, multi-location) justify premium pricing
5. **Compete on value** - Undercut hardware scanners ($1K-5K one-time) and match/beat app-based competitors ($150-400/month)

## Payment Terms & Billing

### Payment Methods
- **Credit Card** (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) - auto-billed monthly/annually
- **ACH/Bank Transfer** - Enterprise tier only, Net 30 terms
- **Invoice** - Enterprise tier only, requires credit application

### Billing Cycle
- **Monthly**: Billed on the 1st of each month, prorated for mid-month starts
- **Annual**: Billed upfront, non-refundable (but transferable to new locations)

### Contract Terms
- **Basic/Pro**: Month-to-month after first 12 months (no long-term commitment)
- **Enterprise**: 12-month minimum, then month-to-month or renew for 10% discount

### Cancellation Policy
- **Notice**: 30 days written notice required
- **Hardware**: Devices on monthly subscription must be returned (prepaid shipping label provided)
- **Data**: Account data retained for 90 days, then permanently deleted (unless otherwise requested)
- **Refunds**: No refunds on monthly subscriptions; annual prepay refunded on prorated basis if canceled within 6 months

## Pricing Strategy Rationale

### Why These Price Points?

1. **Basic ($99)**:
   - **Anchored to competitor floor** (VeriScan starts ~$150, we undercut)
   - **Psychological pricing** ($99 vs. $100 = "under $100/month" messaging)
   - **ROI threshold**: Even smallest bars avoid $2K fine once/year = 16x return

2. **Pro ($299)**:
   - **3x multiplier** over Basic signals premium value
   - **Hyperspectral differentiation** justifies 200% price increase
   - **Sweet spot for mid-market** (most bars/nightclubs fall here)
   - **Matches competitor mid-tier** (PatronScan Pro ~$300) but with unique features

3. **Enterprise ($499)**:
   - **5x multiplier** over Basic for maximum value extraction from chains
   - **API access + multi-location** = high willingness-to-pay (avoid building in-house)
   - **Volume discounts** incentivize chain-wide adoption
   - **Still cheaper than custom development** ($50K+ for DIY solution)

### Price Elasticity Assumptions
- **Basic tier demand**: Highly elastic (small bars price-sensitive) → keep low to maximize adoption
- **Pro tier demand**: Moderately elastic (clubs will pay for accuracy) → optimize for revenue
- **Enterprise demand**: Inelastic (chains have budgets, value integration) → premium pricing

## Pricing FAQ (For Sales Team)

**Q: Why is BullshID cheaper than hardware scanners?**
A: No hardware manufacturing costs, cloud-based = lower overhead. We pass savings to customers.

**Q: Can we negotiate custom pricing?**
A: Enterprise tier (5+ locations) only. Basic/Pro are fixed (fairness to all customers).

**Q: What if we only need it seasonally (college bar during school year)?**
A: Pause subscription for up to 3 months/year (Pro/Enterprise only). Devices returned during pause.

**Q: Do you offer non-profit or government discounts?**
A: Yes, 20% off for verified non-profits, educational institutions, government agencies.

**Q: Can we pay per scan instead of monthly?**
A: Not currently. Per-scan pricing ($0.10-0.50/scan) may be tested in Year 2 for low-volume venues.

**Q: What happens if we outgrow our tier?**
A: Seamless upgrade anytime. Pay prorated difference for remaining monthly term.

**Q: Is there a family/friends discount?**
A: Referral program: Existing customer refers new venue → both get 1 month free.
